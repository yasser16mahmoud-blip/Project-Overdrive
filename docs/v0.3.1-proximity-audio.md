# v0.3.1: nearby-rival audio cue

During an active race, the script checks the distance between participating riders' bikes. When another racer comes within 10 blocks, the rider hears a short built-in Minecraft audio cue. The pitch changes with distance, and a 2-second per-racer cooldown prevents the cue from repeating every tick. It is only active during races and ignores free-ride players, finished racers, and bikes in other dimensions.

This is a lightweight proximity alert, not a bespoke recorded motorcycle pass-by sound. The existing engine sound remains spatially emitted from the bike and naturally becomes louder as it gets closer. In-game multiplayer testing is still needed to check the selected sound event, attenuation, and mix with engine cues.

## Test

1. Update both packs to version 0.3.1 and enable them in a multiplayer test world.
2. Mount two different Overdrive bikes and start with `/scriptevent overdrive:race start`.
3. Ride within 10 blocks of the other participant and confirm each rider hears a subtle cue; move away and back to verify the cooldown.
4. Confirm no proximity cue plays outside a race or for non-participants.
