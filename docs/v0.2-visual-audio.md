# v0.2.1: bike variety, rider gear, effects

## Bike variants

Spawn the three rideable profiles with `/summon overdrive:racer_bike`, `/summon overdrive:sprint_bike`, or `/summon overdrive:tour_bike`. The GP is the balanced base model, Sprint R has a sharper fairing and higher movement value, and Touring X has a wider tank, raised screen, and panniers with a lower movement value. These are distinct entity/model silhouettes and tuning values, not just HUD labels.

## Helmets

Added Carbon, Goldline, and Steel Visor helmet wearable items. They use vanilla helmet icons/material rendering so they remain self-contained without pretending custom helmet textures have been painted. Give them with `/give @s overdrive:helmet_carbon`, `/give @s overdrive:helmet_gold`, or `/give @s overdrive:helmet_iron`.

## Audio and dust

While ridden and moving, each bike plays a restrained speed-pitched Minecraft minecart rolling sound as a temporary engine cue. Gear-estimate changes play a click. A small vanilla smoke particle is emitted behind the bike at a limited interval as a tire/dust placeholder. These are lightweight built-in game effects; original engine recordings, skid/brake sounds, and custom brown dust texture are not included yet.

## Lights

The model includes front and rear lamp meshes. The Bedrock add-on API does not provide a simple portable per-entity dynamic-light toggle or a custom R2 headlight binding; these are currently visible lamp housings rather than a claim of world-illuminating headlamps. Custom emissive texture artwork and runtime lighting remain future work.

## Test

On a fresh world with both packs enabled, check the Content Log, summon each bike variant, compare silhouettes and movement, mount and drive to check the HUD/engine cue/rear particle, and equip each helmet item. Verify that only one sound cue is audible per bike and that particles do not become distracting. The current tool environment cannot launch Minecraft, so an in-game validation is still required.
